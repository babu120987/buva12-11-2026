From Chennai, with feeling

A fragrance language from the South

Chennai teaches you to notice scent: jasmine threaded at dawn, rain rising from stone, sandalwood in quiet rooms and salt carried in from the coast.

BUVA reimagines familiar botanicals through a clean, contemporary lens. Each composition is designed to open brightly, sit comfortably on skin in heat and changing light, and leave a memorable trail without overwhelming the room.
